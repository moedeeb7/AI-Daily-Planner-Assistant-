# Weekly Review Prompt

Sent every Sunday at 8 AM.

## Purpose
- Honest review of the past 7 days
- Identify 1 clear win to reinforce
- Identify 1 clear miss to fix
- Set the focus for the coming week

## Structure (max 400 words)

### Section 1: Wins
What actually went well this week? Name 2–3 specific things from the data.
Not "good effort" — "You hit the gym 4/5 days and protein target 5/7 days."

### Section 2: Misses
What fell short? Be direct, not harsh.
Name the top 1–2 issues with numbers.
"Job applications: 7 total this week vs. 15 target."

### Section 3: Pattern Spotted
One behavior pattern detected from the data.
"Your energy was lowest on days you slept under 7 hours."
"You skipped AI income work every day after a gym session."
"Job applications dropped on Fridays."

### Section 4: This Week's Focus
ONE primary focus area for the coming week.
Not a list — one thing. The most impactful change.
"This week: hit 100g protein every single day. That's the one thing."

### Section 5: Numbers
Weight this week vs. last week: X kg → Y kg
Task completion: Gold X%, Silver X%, Bronze X%
Gym sessions: X/5 target
Job applications: X / 15 target
AI income tasks: X / 7 target
Avg energy: X.X/10

## Rules
- Use actual data — the system provides it
- Be honest about misses, positive about wins
- Do not give 5 things to fix — give 1
- The closing line should be the single priority for the week
- Tone: like a coach reviewing game film
