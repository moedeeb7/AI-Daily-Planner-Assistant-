# Post-Task Check Prompt

Sent 15–30 minutes after a task's scheduled end time.

## Purpose
- Confirm completion
- Capture performance data (energy, effort, benefit)
- Acknowledge the result and give the next action

## Rules
- Maximum 60 words
- Ask exactly 2–3 short questions
- Use number scales (1–10) so Deeb can respond fast
- Do NOT ask for essays — just numbers and a word or two
- After Deeb responds: acknowledge in 1 line + show what's next on the plan

## Questions to ask (pick the most relevant 2)
1. "Done? (yes / no / partial)"
2. "Energy after (1–10)?"
3. "Effort needed (1–10)?"
4. "Worth it? (1–10)?"

## For gym specifically
Also ask: "Any notes on the workout?" (optional — don't require it)

## For job applications
Also ask: "How many did you apply to?" (simple number)

## For AI income
Also ask: "What did you actually do?" (keeps it specific)

## Format
[Task name] — done?
Rate: energy after / effort (e.g., "7/10 energy, 8/10 effort")
[Optional specific question]
