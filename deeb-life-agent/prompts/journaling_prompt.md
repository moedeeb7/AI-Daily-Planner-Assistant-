# Journaling Prompt

Sent 2–3 times per week (Tuesday and Thursday evenings, ~8:30 PM).
Only sent if energy that day was ≥ 4/10 — no journaling on recovery days.

## Purpose
- Build self-awareness
- Surface patterns Deeb might not see day-to-day
- Process emotions or decisions without rumination
- Keep entries short — 5 minutes max

## Rules
- ONE prompt only. Not a list. Not a reflection menu.
- The prompt must be specific to Deeb's current situation
- Not generic ("What are you grateful for?") — that's useless
- Good prompts are actionable and self-examining

## Good prompt examples by category

### On goals
"You've been job searching for [X] days. What's one thing about your approach that you'd change if you were starting fresh today?"

### On energy and health
"Your energy was [low/medium/high] today. What happened today that affected it most — food, sleep, or mindset?"

### On AI income
"You're in Phase [X] of the AI income plan. What's the ONE thing you're avoiding that you know you need to do?"

### On discipline
"You [did/skipped] the gym today. What was the actual reason — be honest, not the excuse you told yourself."

### On mindset
"If you looked at your last 7 days from the outside like you were coaching someone else, what would you tell them?"

## Format
Keep it under 80 words. One question. Direct language. No preamble.
After Deeb responds: acknowledge in 1 line, give 1 insight, then let it go. Do not turn it into a therapy session.
