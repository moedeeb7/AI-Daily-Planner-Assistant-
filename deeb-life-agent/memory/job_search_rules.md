# Job Search Rules — Deeb

Systematic approach to landing a better job within 60 days.

---

## Weekly Targets

| Activity                | Daily (Mon–Fri) | Weekly minimum |
|-------------------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Applications sent       | 3–5             | 15             |
| Follow-ups sent         | 0–1             | 2              |
| New connections (LinkedIn) | 2–3          | 10             |
| Resume/profile updates  | —               | 1 review       |

---

## Application Priority System

**Tier 1 — Apply immediately:**
- Role matches skills 70%+ 
- Company is known/reputable
- Job posted < 7 days ago
- Salary aligns with target

**Tier 2 — Apply with minor tailoring:**
- Role matches skills 50–70%
- Stretch opportunity
- Requires brief cover letter adjustment

**Tier 3 — Skip:**
- Role matches < 50%
- Commission-only or unpaid
- Suspicious or unclear posting

---

## Daily Job Search Block (09:30 – 10:30)

1. Open LinkedIn / Indeed
2. Search new listings posted in last 24 hours
3. Apply to 3 Tier 1 roles using saved resume
4. Send 1 follow-up if interview/response pending
5. Log applications in system ("I applied to X at Y")
6. Stop at 10:30 — do not let it bleed into the afternoon

---

## Resume Rules
- Keep 1 master resume (update weekly)
- Tailor only the summary and top 3 bullets per application
- Use quantified achievements: "reduced load time by 40%" not "improved performance"
- LinkedIn profile must match resume — keep both in sync

---

## Follow-Up Protocol
- Application: follow up after 5–7 days with a short email
- After interview: thank-you note within 24 hours
- No response to follow-up: one more attempt after 5 days, then move on
- Never follow up more than twice for the same role

---

## Tracking in the system
When Deeb says "I applied to [company] for [role]":
1. System logs company, role, date, platform
2. Sets a follow-up reminder for 5 days later
3. Agent acknowledges with: "Logged. Follow-up reminder set for [date]."

When Deeb reports an interview:
1. System updates status to "interview"
2. Agent sends prep checklist: research company, prepare 3 stories, know your numbers

---

## Mindset Rules
- Application count matters more than application quality at this stage — volume + good targeting beats crafting the perfect message for 1 role
- Rejection is data — it tells you to adjust, not quit
- Treat job search like a numbers game: apply to 15 this week, 1–2 will respond
- Do not apply while exhausted — low quality applications waste time

---

## Red Flags (to avoid)
- Companies asking for payment upfront
- Roles with no salary range and vague descriptions
- "We'll reach out" with no contact info
- Too-generic job descriptions
