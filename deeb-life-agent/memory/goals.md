# Goals — Deeb

## Priority Order
1. Gain weight and improve energy levels
2. Build gym consistency (4–5x/week)
3. Land a better job
4. Build AI/business income ($500/month → scale)
5. Develop discipline and sustainable daily habits

---

## Goal 1: Weight Gain
- **Target:** +5 kg in 90 days
- **Method:** Caloric surplus (3000+ kcal/day), 100g+ protein, compound lifts
- **Metric tracked:** Weekly weight (Monday morning, same conditions)
- **Trigger for adjustment:** No weight gain after 2 weeks → add 200 kcal/day
- **Current status:** Tracking from baseline

## Goal 2: Gym Consistency
- **Target:** 4 gym sessions per week minimum
- **Schedule:** Mon / Wed / Fri / Sat preferred
- **Program:** Compound lifts (squat, bench, deadlift, rows, overhead press)
- **Minimum effective session:** 45 minutes
- **Recovery rule:** Never skip more than 2 days in a row
- **Streak target:** 30-day consistent habit by end of month

## Goal 3: Better Job
- **Target:** Accept offer within 60 days
- **Daily minimum:** 3 applications on workdays
- **Weekly minimum:** 1 follow-up email, 1 resume update review
- **Platform priority:** LinkedIn > Indeed > direct outreach
- **Target roles:** AI/tech-adjacent, higher salary, remote or hybrid preferred

## Goal 4: AI / Business Income
- **90-day target:** First $500 earned
- **Phase 1 (weeks 1–4):** Learning — pick 1 AI tool to specialize in, build 1 demo project
- **Phase 2 (weeks 5–8):** Building — create a sellable product or service
- **Phase 3 (weeks 9–12):** Outreach + monetization — first paying client or product sale
- **Daily minimum:** 1 task (learning OR building OR outreach) — no zero days
- **Tracked via:** ai_income_logs in database

## Goal 5: Discipline System
- **Morning routine target:** Awake + moving by 7:30 AM
- **No-zero-day rule:** At least 1 bronze task every single day regardless of energy
- **Evening review:** 5 minutes, every night — non-negotiable
- **Journaling:** 2–3x per week when prompted

---

## Weekly Review Questions (used in Sunday report)
1. Did I hit my protein target 5+ days?
2. Did I complete at least 1 gold task per day?
3. Did I apply to 3+ jobs on each workday?
4. Did I do at least 1 AI income task per day?
5. What's my current weight — am I trending up?
