# Energy Rules — Deeb

Rules for adapting the daily schedule based on Deeb's energy levels.

---

## Energy Scale (1–10)

| Level | Label       | What it means for the day |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|
| 9–10  | Peak        | Deep work possible. Full gym. Multi-hour focus blocks. |
| 7–8   | High        | Standard full day. All tasks as planned. |
| 5–6   | Medium      | Prioritize gold tasks. Shorten deep work sessions to 45 min. |
| 3–4   | Low         | Reduce to essentials: gym (shorter), 1 job app, protein target. |
| 1–2   | Recovery    | Rest day. Protect sleep, eat, walk. Move all cognitive tasks. |

---

## Rules by Energy Level

### High Energy (7–10)
- Schedule gym FIRST — morning session before any other tasks
- Run 2 x 60-minute deep work blocks (job search + AI income)
- Keep meal timing strict — energy burns through calories faster
- This is the best day for hard tasks: resume rewrite, AI project launch, etc.

### Medium Energy (5–6)
- Gym still on — but cap at 60 minutes, skip accessory work if needed
- One 45-minute deep work block, not two
- Job applications in batches of 3 max — stop when focus drops
- Use structured task lists, not open-ended exploration

### Low Energy (3–4)
- Gym becomes a 20-minute walk or light session — do NOT skip entirely
- Job search = 1 application only. Keep the streak alive.
- AI income = watch 1 tutorial or review notes. No building/coding.
- Protein shake mandatory — low energy = likely low appetite
- Evening: sleep by 10 PM, no screens after 9:30 PM

### Recovery (1–2)
- No gym. Rest completely.
- Eat 3 meals minimum regardless of appetite
- One simple habit: 10-minute walk outside
- No job applications or AI work — protect recovery
- Message to send: "Energy is at [X]. Recovery day. Eat, rest, sleep by 10 PM."

## Sleep Impact Rules
- Sleep < 6 hours: treat energy as 2 points lower than reported
- Sleep 6–7 hours: no adjustment
- Sleep 7+ hours: standard
- Sleep < 5 hours: immediate recovery day — no gym, reduce to basics

---

## Energy Recovery Protocol
If energy has been below 5 for 3+ consecutive days:
1. Check sleep patterns (was sleep < 7 for those days?)
2. Check protein (was daily protein below 80g?)
3. Check workout intensity (overtraining?)
4. Recommend: 1–2 rest days + calorie increase + sleep priority
5. Trigger adaptation engine
