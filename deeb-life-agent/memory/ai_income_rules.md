# AI Income Rules — Deeb

Building from zero to first $500/month through AI-powered work.
Strategy: learn one thing deeply → build something real → find first paying customer.

---

## 90-Day Phases

### Phase 1: Weeks 1–4 — Foundation
**Goal:** Pick a lane, build a proof of concept

Daily task: 1 learning session OR 1 building session (45–60 min)

Focus options (pick ONE and commit for 2 weeks minimum):
- AI automation for small businesses (n8n, Make, Zapier + AI)
- AI content creation service (writing, video scripts, social media)
- AI-powered no-code apps (Bubble, Glide, Softr with AI integrations)
- Prompt engineering and ChatGPT/Claude workflow consulting
- AI tools for specific industries (real estate, e-commerce, HR)

Weekly milestone: 1 working demo or 1 completed tutorial series

### Phase 2: Weeks 5–8 — Build
**Goal:** Create something sellable

Daily task: 1 building session (60 min) — no more pure learning

Focus: Build either:
- A productized service (clear scope, fixed price, repeatable)
- A digital product (template, prompt pack, mini-course, tool)
- A client-ready portfolio piece

Weekly milestone: Ship 1 feature or 1 piece of content that proves value

### Phase 3: Weeks 9–12 — Monetize
**Goal:** First $500

Daily task: 1 outreach action + 1 building/maintenance session

Outreach methods (pick 1 daily):
- Post 1 piece of content showing what you built
- Send 3 cold DMs to potential clients
- Post in 1 relevant community offering help
- Respond to 1 relevant job post as a freelancer

Weekly milestone: 1 paid inquiry OR 1 sale OR 1 upwork/fiverr gig created

---

## Daily Minimum (non-negotiable)
**No zero days on AI income.**
Even on bad days: watch 1 YouTube tutorial, read 1 article, take 1 note.
A 15-minute session is better than zero.

---

## Task Classification
When Deeb logs an AI income task, classify it as:
- `learning` — tutorials, courses, research
- `building` — coding, designing, creating
- `testing` — experimenting with tools, running demos
- `outreach` — messaging, posting, contacting
- `monetization` — client meetings, sales, invoicing

---

## Tools to Learn (suggested priority)
1. Claude / ChatGPT API (fundamentals)
2. n8n or Make (automation workflows)
3. Cursor or Bolt (AI coding tools)
4. Midjourney or DALL-E (if creative track)
5. Voiceflow or Botpress (chatbot builders)
6. Supabase + Vercel (if building SaaS)

---

## Income Tracking
- Log every paid transaction immediately
- Log every serious inquiry (might convert)
- Weekly: review what activities led to the most interest
- Adapt: double down on what gets responses, cut what doesn't

---

## Mindset Rules
- Ideas are cheap. Building matters.
- Do not wait to be "ready" — launch an imperfect thing
- The goal in Phase 1 is learning by doing, not consuming endlessly
- Revenue > traffic. Revenue > likes.
- One paying client who gives you $100 is worth more than 1000 followers
