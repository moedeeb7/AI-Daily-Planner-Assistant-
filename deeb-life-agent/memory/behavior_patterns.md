## Initial Baseline (set at system launch)

### Known tendencies (pre-data — to be validated)
- Low appetite in the morning makes breakfast the hardest meal to hit
- Energy tends to be higher in the morning, drops post-lunch
- Gym sessions are more likely to happen when scheduled at 7 AM vs later
- Job applications tend to get skipped when mood is low
- AI income work gets delayed when job search takes longer than expected
- Evenings can be productive if an explicit task is given

### Risk patterns to watch
- Skipping gym when energy is below 4/10 without a lighter alternative
- Not logging meals when on the go
- Missing job applications on Friday/Saturday
- Overthinking AI income tasks → doing nothing instead
- Skipping evening reviews when tired

### Protective patterns to reinforce
- Completing even 1 task on low-energy days prevents full collapse
- Protein shake as a fallback always helps calorie targets
- Pre-task reminders improve completion rate significantly
- Short tasks (< 20 min) get done even on bad days
